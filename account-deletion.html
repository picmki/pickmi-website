In-App Account Deletion Steps

If you currently have the Pickmi app installed on your smartphone, you can also delete your account directly inside the app:

📱 For Pickmi Rider App:

  • 1

    Open Profile Menu

    Launch the Pickmi App and tap your Profile Icon in the upper top bar.

  • 2

    Navigate to Account Settings

    Select Settings > Privacy & Security.

  • 3

    Select Delete Account

    Tap "Delete My Account" at the bottom of the security page.

  • 4

    OTP Authentication

    Enter the 6-digit SMS OTP sent to your registered phone to confirm deletion.

🚕 For Pickmi Driver Partner App:

  • 1

    Set Status to Offline

    Ensure you are not in an active trip and switch your status to OFFLINE.

  • 2

    Go to Account & Documents

    Open Menu > Profile > Account Deletion.

  • 3

    Clear Wallet & Submit

    Ensure all pending payouts are transferred to your bank account before tapping Request Partner Deletion.

Data Deletion & Retention Policy Overview

Data That Will Be Permanently Wiped

  • User Profile: Name, phone number, email address, profile picture, gender, and language preferences.
  • Saved Locations & Favorites: Home, work, and saved trip destinations.
  • Authentication & Tokens: Login tokens, active sessions, push notification tokens, and device identifiers.
  • Driver Documents (for Drivers): Scanned copies of Driving License, Vehicle RC, Vehicle Insurance, and National ID.
  • Ratings & Preferences: Customer feedback ratings and driver vehicle preferences.

Data Retained for Legal Compliance

  • Financial & Tax Records: Invoices, trip billing history, driver payouts, and GST tax records are retained for statutory accounting periods mandated by Indian tax regulations (up to 7 years).
  • Safety & Legal Dispute Logs: If an account is subject to an active safety investigation, fraud report, or legal dispute, relevant logs are temporarily retained until resolution.
  • Anonymized Aggregated Data: De-identified route metrics used for platform performance optimization (stripped of personal identifiers).

⚡ Account Deletion Processing Timeline

Once your deletion request is verified, your account will be immediately deactivated and logged out. The complete deletion of data from our active database servers takes up to 7 to 14 business days. If you change your mind within 7 days, you can contact support at support@pickmicabs.com to cancel the deletion request.
