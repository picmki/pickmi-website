Delete Your PickMi Account

If you would like to delete your PickMi account and associated personal data, you can submit an account deletion request using the form or contact details below.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your PickMi account:

  1. Enter the phone number registered with your PickMi account.
  2. Provide your registered email address, if applicable.
  3. Submit your account deletion request.
  4. Our team will verify the request and process the deletion.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, PickMi will delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account where applicable, including:

  • Account and profile information
  • Registered phone number and email address
  • Saved personal information
  • Account-related preferences
  • Other personal data that is no longer required for legitimate business or legal purposes

Trip and Booking Information

Some information related to completed trips, bookings, payments, invoices, transactions, or other records may need to be retained for a limited period where required for:

  • Legal or regulatory compliance
  • Tax and accounting requirements
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Dispute resolution
  • Financial record keeping

Such information will be retained only for the period required for the applicable purpose and will not be retained longer than necessary.

Processing Time

After receiving and verifying your request, PickMi will process the account deletion request within a reasonable period.

If certain information must be retained for legal, regulatory, accounting, security, or dispute-resolution purposes, that information will be retained for the applicable retention period and deleted or anonymized afterward.

Important

Deleting your PickMi account may permanently remove access to your account, profile, saved information, and other associated services. Account deletion may not be reversible.

If you have an active booking or unresolved payment, refund, or dispute, we may need to resolve the matter before completing deletion of certain associated records.
